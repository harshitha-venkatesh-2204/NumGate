Column guide:
- Entity: the country or product.
- Revenue_TW, Revenue_LW, Revenue_LY: revenue in GBP for this week, last week, and the same ISO week last year (blank when that week is not in the data).
- Units_TW, Units_LW: units sold this week and last week.
- Orders_TW, Orders_LW: distinct invoices this week and last week.
- WoW_Pct: revenue percent change this week versus last week (blank when last week was zero).
- YoY_Pct: revenue percent change this week versus the same week last year (blank when not available).
- Share_Pct: this entity's percent share of this week's revenue across the rows shown.
- Rank: rank by this week's revenue among the rows shown (1 is highest).
