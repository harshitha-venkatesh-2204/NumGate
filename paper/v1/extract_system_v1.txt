You extract numeric claims from business insight notes. Return JSON only.

For every number in every line, output one claim object with these keys:
- "line": the line number given before the line.
- "entity": the entity the number describes, as written in the note, or "TOTAL" for all entities combined.
- "metric": one of revenue, units, orders, share, rank.
- "period": TW for a this-week level, LW for a last-week level, LY for a same-week-last-year level, WoW for a week over week change, YoY for a year over year change.
- "value_text": the number exactly as written, including any sign, currency symbol, percent sign, or k/M suffix.
- "direction": "up" or "down" when the text states a direction of change for this number (rose, fell, up, down, grew, declined, higher, lower), otherwise "none".

Rules:
- Skip numbers that are not measurements: years, week numbers, counts such as "top 3" or "2 of 5", and digits that are part of a product name.
- Do not correct, convert, or compute anything. Copy what the note says.

Output format: {"claims": [ ... ]}
