Methods

Data. UCI Online Retail II, cleaned (cancelled invoices, non-positive quantity or price, missing country and non-product stock codes removed), aggregated to ISO weeks. Each table lists entities with this week, last week and same-week-last-year revenue, units and orders, plus WoW, YoY, share and rank computed in pandas. 60 tables were built (seed 42):

size entity_type tables
large product 20
medium product 20
small country 10
small product 10

Run settings. Strategies S1, S2, S3, S4; 2 tables per size; 2 repeats per condition; temperature 0 where the model accepts it; max 16000 output tokens; S3 at most 2 revision rounds; S2 scripts time out after 30 seconds with one retry. Budget cap $20.0.

name provider model_id price_in_per_M price_out_per_M
claude-opus-5 anthropic claude-opus-5 5 25

Strategies. S1 Direct: the table as markdown, one call. S2 Compute-then-write: the model writes a pandas script that computes up to 25 facts, the script runs in a subprocess, and a second call writes the note from the facts only. S3 Write-then-verify: the S1 draft is gated and flagged numbers are sent back for correction, up to 2 rounds. S4 Facts-template: a deterministic facts packet narrated with every number copied verbatim.

Gate. Level 1 labels each number Supported_cell, Supported_derived (fixed operation library), Unsupported, or Skipped (years, week labels, counting words, digits inside entity names), using a rounding-aware match with 0.5 percent tolerance for k, M and B values. Level 2 extracts (entity, metric, period, value, direction) claims (claude-haiku-4-5 for real models, a rule-based extractor for the mock) and labels them Correct, Wrong_entity, Wrong_metric, Wrong_value or Unverifiable. Full rules: docs/method.md.

Column guide:
- Entity: the country or product.
- Revenue_TW, Revenue_LW, Revenue_LY: revenue in GBP for this week, last week, and the same ISO week last year (blank when that week is not in the data).

- Rank: rank by this week's revenue among the rows shown (1 is highest).
extract_system.txt
You extract numeric claims from business insight notes. Return JSON only.

For every number in every line, output one claim object with these keys:
- "line": the line number given before the line.
- "entity": the single entity the number describes, as written in the note. Use "TOTAL" for all entities combined. Use "GROUP" for a figure about several entities together or a subset, such as "the top five", "the three parasol lines combined", "hot water bottle lines totalled £12,111" or "the six LANDMARK FRAME lines"; use "TOTAL" only for all rows of the table.
- "metric": one of revenue, units, orders, share, rank.
- "period": the period of this number itself. TW for a this-week level, LW for a last-week level, LY for a same-week-last-year level, WoW for a week over week change, YoY for a year over year change. A money amount or count written next to LW or LY (for example "£2,729.22 LW") is a level with period LW or LY, even when a WoW or YoY change follows it.
- "value_text": the number exactly as written, including any sign, currency symbol, percent sign, k/M suffix, or x for ratios.
- "direction": "up" or "down" only when this number is itself a change (period WoW or YoY) and the text states its direction (rose, fell, up, down, grew, declined, higher, lower). For levels (TW, LW, LY), shares and ranks, direction is "none", even in "fell to £1,632.90 TW".
- "qualifier": "approx" when the text hedges the number (about, around, nearly, roughly), "over" for more than or above, "under" for less than or below, otherwise "none".

Rules:
- Skip numbers that are not measurements: years, week numbers, counts such as "top 3" or "2 of 5", and digits that are part of a product name.
- When a sentence lists values for several entities in order ("gains of 22.9% and 86.6% respectively" after naming two entities), give each value to its own entity in that order.
- Do not correct, convert, or compute anything. Copy what the note says.
