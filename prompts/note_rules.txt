Format rules for the note:
- Write 6 to 8 bullet lines. Start each line with "- " and state exactly one insight per line.
- Every line names at least one entity from the data and contains at least one number.
- Label every period explicitly: TW (this week), LW (last week), LY (same ISO week last year), WoW (week over week change), YoY (year over year change).
- Revenue is in GBP. Write money with the £ sign.
- State facts only. Do not give recommendations, advice, or explanations of causes.
- Plain text only. No title, no headings, no markdown tables, no bold, no text before or after the bullets.
- Do not use em dashes.
